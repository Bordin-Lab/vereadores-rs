# Release notes — v0.2.0

## Scientific changes

- Expanded the manuscript from the funding-source/concentration/coupling panel to a four-link analysis: resources, expenditures, nominal votes/list strength, and election.
- Added evidence that revenue–vote alignment in Pelotas remained stable from 2016 to 2024.
- Added statewide fixed-effect attenuation models showing that current votes and list strength absorb 74.8–93.2% of the revenue coefficient.
- Added repeated cross-validation separating previous-election viability from current post-election information.
- Added strict digital-expense harmonization, expenditure-category AUCs, and out-of-sample digital-increment tests.
- Reinterpreted Pelotas's raw high coupling after candidate-count adjustment: the city is exceptional in competition size, not in size-adjusted AUC.
- Added a new four-panel mechanism figure (Fig. 4 in the current manuscript) and expanded the Supplementary Information with complete mechanism tables and safeguards.

## Reproducibility changes
